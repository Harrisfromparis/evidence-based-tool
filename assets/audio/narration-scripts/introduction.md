# Introduction Narration Script

## Welcome Message

Welcome to Autism and Me - an evidence-based practice navigator for autism support in Irish educational settings.

This toolkit provides practical guidance on 29 evidence-based practices, real-world case studies from Irish schools, and planning tools to support autistic students.

All content is written in plain language with a neuro-affirming, rights-based approach.

## What You'll Find Here

**Evidence-Based Practice Library**: Explore 29 research-supported approaches for autism support. Each practice includes clear explanations, implementation guidance, Irish examples, and ethical considerations.

**Case Studies**: Read real-world examples from Irish primary and secondary schools showing how EBPs are used in practice.

**Rights and Ethics Hub**: Access information about the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities, Irish legal frameworks, and ethical guidelines for autism support.

**Planning Tools**: Use practical tools including lesson generators, transition planners, parent assessments, and behaviour reflection frameworks.

## Using This Toolkit

- **Search**: Use the search function to find specific practices or topics
- **Bookmark**: Save items you want to return to quickly
- **Audio Narration**: Enable text-to-speech to have content read aloud
- **Offline Access**: All content works offline once loaded

## Our Approach

This toolkit is grounded in:
- **Evidence**: All practices are research-supported
- **Rights**: We centre the UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities
- **Neuro-affirmation**: We respect autistic ways of being
- **Plain language**: No jargon, clear explanations
- **Irish context**: Examples from Irish educational settings

## Getting Started

Start by browsing the EBP Library to explore the 29 evidence-based practices, or jump straight to the Tools section if you have a specific planning task in mind.

Remember: you are not required to create an account. Your data stays on your device.
