# Home-School Communication Sheet Template

## Purpose
Facilitate consistent, structured communication between home and school to ensure coordinated support.

## Format Options
- Daily communication book
- Weekly email summary
- Digital app entry
- Structured form

## Layout Rules
- Brief, structured sections (not essays)
- Checkboxes and scales for quick completion
- Space for open-ended notes when needed
- Professional, factual tone
- Solution-focused language
- Space for both positive and challenging information

## Communication Principles
- Timely - complete same day when possible
- Balanced - share successes alongside challenges
- Factual - observations, not assumptions
- Respectful - collaborative team approach
- Confidential - protect student privacy
- Actionable - include relevant, useful information
- Consistent - complete regularly as agreed

## Frequency Options
- **Daily**: For students needing close home-school coordination
- **Weekly**: Summary of week with key observations
- **As-needed**: For specific events or concerns

## Accessibility Notes
- Clear, simple language
- Structured format for easy completion
- Option for digital or paper
- Brief enough to be sustainable
- Can be adapted to family's communication preferences
- Translation available if needed

## Implementation Tips
- Agree on format and frequency at start
- Keep it manageable - brevity is key
- Use shorthand or codes if helpful
- Acknowledge entries from other party
- Review system regularly - is it working?
- Adjust format as needs change
- Remember: collaboration, not reporting

## What to Avoid
- Lengthy narratives (keep it brief)
- Blame or judgement
- Jargon or technical language
- Sharing only problems (include positives)
- Using it as only communication method (supplement with meetings)
- Making it burdensome (keep it practical)
