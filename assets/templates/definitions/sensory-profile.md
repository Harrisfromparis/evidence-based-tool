# Sensory Profile Template

## Purpose
Document how a student responds to different types of sensory input to guide environmental modifications and accommodations.

## Layout Rules
- Use clear section headers
- Provide adequate space for handwritten or typed observations
- Include checkboxes for common sensory responses
- Allow space for individualised notes
- Use tables or structured lists for easy completion

## Accessibility Notes
- Large, readable font (minimum 14pt)
- High contrast
- Clear section breaks
- Option for digital or paper completion
- Can be completed collaboratively with student, family, and school team
