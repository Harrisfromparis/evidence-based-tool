# Behaviour Support Plan Template

## Purpose
Create a comprehensive, positive, evidence-based plan for understanding and supporting behaviour that is challenging.

## Sections

### Student Information
- Name
- Age / Class
- Date plan created
- Plan created by (names and roles)
- Review date
- Distribution list (who has copy of this plan)

### Understanding the Behaviour

**Describe the behaviour**
- Observable, specific description (what does it look like?)
- Avoid judgement or interpretation
- Include frequency, duration, intensity

**Functional Behaviour Assessment Summary**
- When does the behaviour most often occur? (time, activity, setting)
- What typically happens right before? (antecedent)
- What typically happens right after? (consequence)
- What is the behaviour communicating? (function)
  ☐ Seeking attention/connection
  ☐ Escaping/avoiding (demand, sensory, social)
  ☐ Accessing preferred items/activities
  ☐ Meeting sensory need

**Additional context**
- Sensory factors
- Social demands
- Task difficulty
- Wellbeing factors (sleep, hunger, health)
- Patterns or triggers

### Prevention Strategies (Antecedent-Based)

**Instructional strategies**

- Predictability and routine

**Proactive supports**

- Sensory tools

### Response Strategies

**When behaviour occurs, we will:**

**Immediate response** (keep everyone safe)
- Safety procedures if needed
- Calm, neutral adult response
- Minimal attention to behaviour itself

**After behaviour has de-escalated**
- Acknowledge student's feelings
- Support use of alternative communication/coping strategy
- Return to activity or provide break as appropriate
- Process with student when calm (if appropriate)

**What we will NOT do**
- Punish or shame
- Escalate situation with own emotional response
- Remove previously-earned privileges
- Force immediate processing during distress

## Layout Rules
- Clear section headers with adequate space
- Professional but accessible language
- Focus on understanding and support, not control
- Collaborative document - input from all team members
- Specific, actionable strategies
- Positive framing throughout

## Accessibility Notes
- Large, readable font
- High contrast
- Structured format
- Option for digital or paper version
- Quick reference version for staff to carry
- Include visual supports where helpful

## Ethical Considerations
- Centre student dignity and autonomy
- Behaviour as communication framework
- No punishment-based approaches
- Focus on teaching skills, not suppressing behaviour
- Environmental modification before student modification
- Trauma-informed approach
- Respect for student's neurodivergence
- Collaboration with family essential
- Regular review and adjustment
