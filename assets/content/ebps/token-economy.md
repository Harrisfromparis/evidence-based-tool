# Token Economy

## What it is

System where students earn tokens (points, stickers, tickets) for desired behaviours, then exchange accumulated tokens for preferred items or activities. Token systems provide visible, tangible representation of progress toward goals.

## Why it matters

Token economies provide clear feedback about performance, allow delayed reinforcement whilst maintaining motivation, and give students agency in choosing their own reinforcers from a menu of options.

## How to implement

1. **Identify target behaviours**: Choose specific, observable behaviours to reinforce
2. **Select tokens**: Choose tangible items (stickers, tickets, points on chart) that are easy to deliver immediately
3. **Create reinforcer menu**: List items/activities students can "purchase" with tokens, with varying costs
4. **Establish exchange rate**: Determine how many tokens = which reinforcers
5. **Deliver tokens immediately**: Give tokens right after desired behaviour occurs
6. **Allow regular exchanges**: Let students trade tokens for reinforcers frequently, especially initially
7. **Fade system gradually**: Work toward natural reinforcement over time

### Practical examples

- Sticker chart: Earn sticker for each completed assignment; 5 stickers = choice of preferred activity
- Class token system: Earn points for following expectations; points can be traded for privileges or items
- Positive behaviour tickets: Teacher gives tickets throughout day; Friday raffle for prizes
- Individual token board: Earn tokens for specific goals (asking for help, completing work); exchange for break time, preferred activity, or tangible item

## Good to know

- **Individual differences**: What motivates one student may not motivate another
- **Start with easy goals**: Students should earn tokens frequently at first to build motivation
- **Avoid token loss**: Taking away earned tokens is usually counterproductive
- **Fair access**: Ensure all students can realistically earn tokens
- **Fade when appropriate**: Work toward intrinsic motivation and natural consequences

## Evidence base

Token economies have extensive research support showing effectiveness for increasing desired behaviours in educational settings. Most effective when tokens are delivered immediately, reinforcers are individually chosen, and system is implemented consistently.
