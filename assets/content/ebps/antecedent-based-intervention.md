# Antecedent-Based Intervention

## What it is

Modifying the environment or activity before behaviour occurs to prevent challenges and promote engagement. This proactive approach addresses potential triggers before they lead to difficulties.

## Why it matters

Prevention is more effective and respectful than responding to distress after it occurs. By adjusting environmental factors, we reduce the need for students to communicate through challenging behaviour and create conditions where they can succeed.

## How to implement

1. **Identify patterns**: Observe when and where the student struggles most
2. **Analyse antecedents**: What happens immediately before difficulties arise?
3. **Modify one factor**: Change lighting, noise levels, seating position, timing, or task demands
4. **Observe outcomes**: Does the modification reduce difficulties?
5. **Refine approach**: Adjust based on the student's response

### Practical examples

- Move a student's desk away from a noisy corridor door
- Provide a visual schedule before activities that typically cause anxiety
- Offer noise-cancelling headphones during assembly
- Give advance warnings (5-minute, 2-minute) with visual timers before transitions
- Incorporate student's special interests into lesson materials
- Allow choice between two acceptable options to increase sense of control

## Good to know

- **Start small**: Modify one thing at a time so you can identify what works
- **Student input is essential**: Ask the student what helps or observe their preferences
- **Avoid segregation**: Modifications should support inclusion, not isolate the student
- **Review regularly**: What helps may change over time as the student develops

## Evidence base

Antecedent-based interventions are supported by extensive research showing effectiveness in reducing challenging behaviours and increasing engagement across all age groups and settings. This approach aligns with positive behaviour support principles and is recommended by the National Autism Center.

**Related practices**: Visual Supports, Self-Management, Structured Work Systems
