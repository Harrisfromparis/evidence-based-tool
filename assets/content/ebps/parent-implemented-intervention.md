# Parent-Implemented Intervention

## What it is

Teaching parents and caregivers to use evidence-based strategies at home and in community settings. Parents become skilled interventionists, embedding learning and support into daily family life.

## Why it matters

Parents spend more time with their children than any professional. When families have strategies and confidence, support becomes consistent across all environments, leading to better outcomes and reduced family stress.

## How to implement

1. **Collaborate with families**: Ask what goals and challenges matter most to them
2. **Teach strategies explicitly**: Show, model, and practise EBPs with parents
3. **Use video feedback**: Record parents using strategies and review together
4. **Start with manageable goals**: Choose one or two approaches rather than overwhelming families
5. **Support and problem-solve**: Regular check-ins to address challenges and celebrate successes
6. **Respect family context**: Adapt strategies to fit family routines, values, and resources

### Practical examples

- Teach parents to use visual schedules during morning and evening routines
- Show families how to use naturalistic teaching during play and mealtimes
- Support parents in implementing consistent communication system (AAC) at home
- Coach families on environmental modifications that reduce sensory overwhelm
- Teach parents functional communication training strategies

## Good to know

- **Avoid parent-blaming**: Frame support as partnership, not correction of parent mistakes
- **Acknowledge family stress**: Many families are exhausted and overwhelmed
- **Cultural responsiveness**: Strategies must align with family values and cultural context
- **Build on strengths**: Recognise what families are already doing well
- **Practical and realistic**: Strategies must fit into real family life, not ideal laboratory conditions

## Evidence base

Parent-implemented interventions have substantial research support showing improved child outcomes, increased parent confidence, reduced family stress, and better generalisation of skills across settings. Coaching models with video feedback are particularly effective.

**Related practices**: Naturalistic Intervention, Functional Communication Training, Collaborative Problem-Solving
