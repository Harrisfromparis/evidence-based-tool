# Goal Planner Tool

## Purpose

A structured goal-planning tool helps educators, families, and students identify meaningful goals and create action plans for achieving them. Effective goals are specific, measurable, and genuinely important to the student's wellbeing and development.

## Who it is for

- Teachers planning IEP or support goals
- Families setting home-based goals
- Students working toward self-identified objectives
- Support teams coordinating interventions
- Professionals monitoring progress

## How to use

1. **Identify broad goal area**: What aspect of development or functioning will this goal address?
2. **Make it specific**: Turn general aim into concrete, observable target
3. **Ensure it's meaningful**: Will achieving this goal genuinely improve the student's life?
4. **Set success criteria**: How will you know the goal is achieved?
5. **Break into steps**: Divide goal into smaller, achievable milestones
6. **Identify supports needed**: What EBPs, accommodations, or resources will help?
7. **Assign responsibilities**: Who will do what?
8. **Set timeline**: When will progress be reviewed?
9. **Monitor and adjust**: Regular check-ins to assess progress and modify approach

### Goal categories

- Communication and language
- Social understanding and relationships
- Academic skills
- Daily living and independence
- Emotional regulation
- Sensory needs
- Transition support
- Community participation

### Questions to ensure meaningful goals

- Does this goal matter to the student (not just adults)?
- Will achieving this improve the student's quality of life?
- Is this developmentally appropriate?
- Have we considered the student's perspective?
- Are we addressing skill development or just behaviour control?
- Does this goal respect the student's neurodivergence?

## Notes

- Involve the student in goal-setting whenever possible
- Avoid goals that aim to make students "less autistic" or more compliant
- Focus on building skills, not suppressing harmless differences
- Review goals regularly - needs and priorities change
- Celebrate progress, not just achievement of final goal
- Consider whether environmental changes might be more appropriate than teaching the student to adapt
- Ensure goals align with rights-based, neuro-affirming practice
